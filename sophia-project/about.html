About North Star Bakery

Our story

North star bakery began as a small neighborhood bakery aiming to sell delicious treats for the residents. What started small has turned into hit spot where our customers feel at home.

Our way

We believe in only serving food we'd feed our loved ones. North star is focused on not only on tasty treats but high quality ingredients you can feel safe consuming.
